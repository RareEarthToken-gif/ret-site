About

Rare Earth Token (RET) is an independent digital-asset project with a publicly identifiable founder.

Its connection to rare earth elements is conceptual and thematic. Rare earths provide strategic and technological context for the project, while RET itself remains a blockchain-native ERC-20 digital asset.

RET does not represent, track, redeem for, or provide ownership of physical rare earth elements or other commodities. It does not grant equity, dividend, profit-sharing, debt, redemption or holder-governance rights.

RET does not use the price, market value, inventory or ownership of rare earth elements as a mechanism for determining or supporting the value of the token.

How RET Operates

RET was deployed with 30,000,000 tokens created once by the constructor. The deployed contract contains no mechanism capable of creating additional RET after deployment.

Treasury RET may be distributed through owner-controlled on-chain functions. The project does not state a fixed future treasury distribution schedule on this website.

RET does not guarantee a market price, minimum value, liquidity, market demand or financial return. If secondary-market trading becomes available, market conditions may include supply, demand and available liquidity.

Smart contract

RET is deployed on Ethereum Mainnet. Compiler: Solidity 0.8.30+commit.73712a01, optimization disabled, MIT license, source code verified as an exact match on Etherscan.

  • Standard ERC-20 functionality
  • burn() / burnFrom() — supply reduction
  • pause() / unpause() — global pause control
  • distribute() — treasury distribution
  • rescueTokens() — token recovery utility
  • sweepETH() — ETH recovery utility
  • transferOwnership() / renounceOwnership() — administrative ownership controls

The contract contains centralized administrative controls. Owner-level functions are controlled by the Ethereum address returned by owner(). The founder identity and the technical owner address are distinct concepts.

The owner cannot mint additional RET, impose a transfer tax, rebase holder balances, blacklist an individual wallet, or directly seize RET from holder wallets through a special owner function. The deployed RET contract contains no upgrade or proxy mechanism.
